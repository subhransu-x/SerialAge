Data Plate

Where to find the serial number

The serial number is located on the manufacturer's data plate (rating plate). For outdoor AC or heat pump units, it is usually on the side or back of the cabinet above the refrigerant valves. For indoor furnaces or air handlers, look inside the front access panel.

Format Guide

Supported Formats

Modern Standard (10-Character)Synthetic
4206A12345

Used from ~1985 to present. The first two digits encode the week (01–52), and the next two digits encode the year.

Legacy (9-Digit)Synthetic
851212345

Used during the 1980s. The first two digits encode the year (80-89), and the next two digits encode the month.

Keep in mind

Limitations

  • Serial numbers manufactured before approximately 1985 (pre-Style 1) cannot be reliably decoded due to inconsistent historical formatting.
  • Week-based dates (WWYY format) are precise to within 7 days, not a specific calendar date.
  • Does not support Carrier water heaters.
  • If a printed "MFR DATE" appears on the data plate, always trust it over the decoded result.
