Ruud Serial Number Decoder

Determine the age and manufacture date of your Ruud HVAC equipment. Ruud operates under Rheem and uses the exact same serial number formats, including the standard 10-character layout and embedded plant code formats.

Data Plate

Where to find the serial number

The serial number is located on the manufacturer's data plate (rating plate). For outdoor AC or heat pump units, it is usually on the side or back of the cabinet. For indoor furnaces, look inside the front access panel.

Keep in mind

Limitations

  • Ruud water heater serial numbers (typically 10 all-numeric digits) are not supported by this HVAC decoder.
  • Serial numbers less than 10 characters long are not supported.
  • The older embedded plant-code format date extraction is based on plant letter position. Always verify against the unit data plate if unsure.
